Email Fetcher & Drive Uploader

This service automatically scans Gmail for configured label/sender combinations, fetches the raw email messages, and uploads them as .eml files into Google Drive folders for archiving and downstream automation.

Health Check

The cron job and uptime probes should hit /health by default.

Deployment Note

Render should call the service at the root URL, which now serves this landing page.

Cron Timing

Schedule the recurring cron job for 6:00 AM every day.

What It Does

  • Reads Gmail labels and sender mappings
  • Downloads the raw email body as .eml
  • Stores files in the configured Google Drive folder
If you see this page on Render, the service is running correctly. For the exact job status, check /health and the Render logs.
